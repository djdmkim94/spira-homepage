# TikTok Shop Prospect Intelligence — US Market

**Prepared for:** Spira BD/Partnerships & Leadership
**Scope:** 10 TikTok Shop (TTS) categories, US market
**Method:** 20-agent research workflow (~421 web searches) with an adversarial verification pass on every decision-maker
**Compiled:** 2026-07-03
**Companion files:** [`brands.csv`](./brands.csv) (full per-brand schema) · [`agencies.csv`](./agencies.csv) · [`bottomline.md`](./bottomline.md)

---

## 1. Executive Summary

We mapped **79 TTS-active US brands and 37 servicing agencies** across all 10 target categories to prioritize Spira's BD outreach. Spira's wedge — on-brand short-form content, AI influencer/UGC creative, and creator/social workflows — maps directly onto the content volume TTS brands burn through weekly.

**Headline findings:**

- **70% of brands (55/79) are WINNABLE** — TTS-native or mid-market, high content velocity, and no evidence of a locked-in holding-company agency. These are real pipeline, not just market-map names.
- **Fit is nearly universal: 97% of brands score 4–5/5** on "needs high-velocity content + creator marketing" (average 4.47). Half score a perfect 5.
- **18 brands are outreach-ready today** — WINNABLE, top-fit, *and* with a decision-maker whose LinkedIn we independently verified.
- **No fabrication:** 36 KDMs are HIGH-confidence with a verified LinkedIn URL; 29 were deliberately left LOW/blank because the individual could not be independently confirmed. Every URL in the dataset was seen in a real source.
- **Agencies are mostly partners, not rivals:** 29 of 37 are TikTok-Shop-management / TSP shops and 19 do influencer/UGC work — ~78% are candidate channel or white-label partners that need exactly the content volume Spira produces.

## 2. Market Size

*Verified market weight vs. modeled Spira opportunity — kept separate so each can be defended independently. Revenue figures were publicly reported for 47 of 79 brands; the remainder are banded estimates.*

| Metric | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Combined revenue, all 79 mapped brands | **~$10.5B** | Reported figures + extrapolation |
| Combined revenue, 11 mega-flagships | ~$5.4B | Reported (Anker, Cirkul, Solo Stove, Jackery, Naterra, etc.) |
| **Combined revenue, 55 WINNABLE brands** | **~$4.1B** | The serviceable base we sell into |
| **SAM — annual content + creator spend (winnable base)** | **~$250M/yr** | Modeled @ ~6% of revenue |
| **SOM — Year-1 ARR capturable from this list** | **~$1.2M–$1.7M** | Model (see levers below) |

**SOM model levers (adjust live):** 15–25% win rate of the 55 winnable brands (8–14 logos) × $40K–$75K blended ACV = $0.6M–$1.1M ARR, plus ~$0.6M from winning 5 of 37 agencies as white-label partners at ~$120K each. Winnable brands cluster in a **$20M–$200M revenue band** — big enough to have budget, small enough to lack an agency of record.

## 3. Where to Hunt

| Signal | Categories | Play |
|---|---|---|
| **Deepest winnable pools** | Menswear (7), Home Supplies (6), Food & Bev (6), Automotive (6) | Volume outreach — founder/director-led, no AOR |
| **Highest content hunger** | Health (avg fit 4.8), Beauty (4.7) | Lead with content-velocity ROI |
| **Most open (sell direct)** | Home, Phones, Automotive, Fashion Accessories (3 agencies each) | Direct-to-brand, little agency friction |
| **Agency-dense (co-sell / white-label)** | Food & Bev (5), then Beauty/Womenswear/Menswear/Sports/Health (4 each) | Partner motion alongside direct |

**Fastest-converting archetype:** founder- or affiliate-director-led brands running daily TikTok Lives + large affiliate armies (Canvas Beauty, goPure, Divi, Popilush, YoungLA). Content velocity is existential and one reachable person owns the budget.
